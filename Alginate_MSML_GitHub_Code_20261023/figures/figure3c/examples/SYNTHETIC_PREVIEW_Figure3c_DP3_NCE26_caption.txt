Fig. 3c | Global rank-two NMF weight trends for DP3 at NCE 26%. Each marker represents the mean NMF weight of the selected precursor spectra from one of seven NMR-calibrated fraction mixtures. Error bars indicate ±1 standard deviation across the 75–75 retained technical MS/MS scans per mixture (counts listed in source-data table). Components are ordered descriptively by correlation with M fraction; W1 and W2 are display labels, not chemical species. NMF fitting itself uses no composition labels. Technical-scan error bars do not quantify between-preparation variability.
